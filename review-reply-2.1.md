2. Purpose and target audience
Tokaeshi (十返し) is an offline, turn-based board game for one or two players, a numeric variant of Reversi on an 8x8 board. Each piece shows a number from 1 to 9; when a piece is flipped it changes owner and its value becomes 10 minus the original. You flip an opponent's line of pieces only when the total of your pieces sandwiching it exceeds the opponent's total. There are also two special pieces, a Tank and a Bomb. The player with more pieces on the board at the end wins. It is aimed at general audiences who enjoy strategy and puzzle games, from casual players to Reversi fans. It provides a new layer of arithmetic and tactical decisions on top of a familiar game.

3. How to use the main features
No login, credentials, or sample files are required. From the title screen:
- "2人で対戦": two players play on the same device, taking turns.
- "CPUと対戦（自分が先行）" / "CPUと対戦（自分が後攻）": play against the built-in CPU as the first or second player.
- "遊び方を見る": an interactive tutorial that explains each rule step by step on a sample board.
In a game, select a piece from your hand at the bottom of the screen and tap a highlighted cell. The first 6 moves are a setup phase on the center cells. When a Bomb is flipped, its owner chooses "上下左右" (orthogonal) or "斜め" (diagonal), and the opponent's piece on the adjacent cell in each of those four directions is flipped.

7. In-App Purchase
One non-consumable product, "広告を削除" (Remove Ads, jp.andygrave.tokaeshi.removeads). It permanently removes all ads (interstitial and banner). To reach it, open the app; the "広告を削除" button with the price is on the title screen, next to "購入を復元" (Restore Purchases). After a purchase or restore, ads stop and both buttons disappear.
